# Task Bar

A themed taskbar for Windows 11 that lives on your **side monitor**, without making that monitor your main display. From the Command Deck family.

- **Start** opens every app on your PC, A to Z, plus your pinned apps.
- **Search** works like the Windows search box: apps, Windows settings pages, your files (from the Windows search index) and the web. Arrow keys to choose, Enter to open.
- **Pinned apps** and **open windows**: one button per window. Click to switch, click the active one to minimize, middle-click to close, right-click to pin.
- **Weather**, **Wi-Fi / volume / notifications** buttons, and a **clock** (click it for the calendar).
- **Seven themes**: Command, Horizon, Hi-Vis, Overclock, Afterhours, Moonbase '79 and Skydock. Change your wallpaper and Task Bar suggests the theme that matches it, or tints the theme's accent to your background.

## Install
1. Download **Task-Bar-Setup-x.y.z.exe** from [Releases](https://github.com/spamadrienneswiss-creator/Task-bar/releases/latest).
2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. The bar appears on your side monitor. Windows' own taskbar tucks itself away (it still slides up if you push the mouse to the bottom of the main screen).

Right-click an empty spot on the bar for the theme list, settings and Quit. Quitting or uninstalling puts the Windows taskbar back the way it was. Updates download by themselves.

## Settings
- **Theme**: pick a look, see your wallpaper's colors and best match, tint, suggestions on or off.
- **Pinned apps**: reorder, remove, add.
- **Weather**: your city and °F / °C.
- **Bar**: which monitor, tuck away the Windows taskbar, start with Windows, 24-hour clock, seconds, web search engine, updates.
